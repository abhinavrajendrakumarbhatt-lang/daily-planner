Features

Time Budget Bar

Available hours at a glance. Red when over-committed.

Drag & Drop

Reorder by priority. Drag between today and backlog.

Duration Picker

15m, 30m, 1h, 2h, 4h. Slots stack sequentially.

Calendar Blocking

Google Calendar events with one click per task.

Initiatives

Tag tasks by project. Add, rename, delete your own.

Daily Auto-Reset

Unfinished tasks roll to backlog. Fresh start every day.

Your data is 100% private — stored in your browser only. Nobody else can see your tasks.
